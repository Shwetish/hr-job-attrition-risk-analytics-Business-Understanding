# hr-job-attrition-risk-analytics-Business-Understanding
CRISP-DM Business Understanding framework for quantifying job hazard levels, workplace burnout risks, candidate pipeline scarcity, and compensation offset strategies.

# ⚠️ Job Risk Profile, Burnout & High-Hazard Talent Attraction Analytics

## 📌 1. Business Problem Statement
> *"If the job is boring, hazardous, tension ridden, and lacking in opportunities for advancement, very few people may be available for such jobs."*

---

## 💡 2. Context & Problem Identification
* **The Issue:** Roles characterized by repetitive monotony, hazardous conditions, high stress, or stagnant career progression face severe talent shortages, high recruitment friction, and extreme early-stage employee attrition. Organizations struggle to fill these critical positions without understanding the exact structural factors driving candidate hesitation.
* **The Solution:** Develop a predictive machine learning framework that evaluates job postings, workplace condition metrics, task volatility, and career pathing structures to compute a multi-factor "Job Friction Score." The model correlates job design traits with applicant pool sizes, predicting necessary compensation premiums, safety enhancements, and career mobility incentives required to stabilize talent pipelines.

---

## 📊 3. Business Objectives & Constraints

### 🎯 Business Objectives
* **Maximize Applicant Fill Rate for High-Friction Roles:** Re-engineer job requirements and incentive structures to ensure adequate talent pipelines for difficult-to-fill positions.
* **Maximize Employee Retention & Stability:** Identify stress, safety, and stagnation triggers early to implement retention interventions before burnout leads to resignation.
* **Minimize Early Attrition & Recruitment Churn:** Reduce 90-day turnover rates in high-stress or hazardous operational roles.

### ⚠️ Constraints & Limitations
* **Minimize Operational Safety & Compliance Risks:** Ensure physical risk and workplace hazard reductions strictly adhere to occupational health and safety (OSHA) regulations.
* **Minimize Uncontrolled Payroll Inflation:** Balance risk-compensation premiums with realistic budget constraints to avoid unsustainable wage structures.

---

## 👥 4. Stakeholder Expectations
* **Talent Acquisition & Recruiting Teams:** Require early warning indicators on job specifications that suffer from low candidate availability.
* **Workplace Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) Teams:** Expect data-backed prioritized lists of physical hazards and high-stress environments needing ergonomic or safety remediation.
* **HR Business Partners & Compensation Teams:** Require clear benchmarks for hazard/stress pay differentials and structured career advancement pathways.
* **Operations Managers:** Require steady workforce capacity without sudden operational shutdowns caused by staffing shortages in hazardous or grueling roles.

---

## ⚙️ 5. Feasibility Assessment
* **Technical Feasibility:** High. Feature importance analysis, survival analysis (for turnover forecasting), classification models (predicting applicant drop-off), and NLP job-description scoring are well-established methodology choices.
* **Data Feasibility:** High. Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), exit interview logs, internal mobility records, safety incident logs, and workplace stress survey telemetry provide actionable historical data.
